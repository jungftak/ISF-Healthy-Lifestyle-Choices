Nutrition: Fuel Your Brain!

What you eat has a huge impact on how you feel, how well you concentrate and how much energy you have to learn and play! 🍎

👇 Tap any card to reveal an amazing fact!

A note about fast food in Hong Kong 🇭🇰
Hong Kong is home to many popular fast food restaurants, including McDonald's, KFC and Café de Coral. These can be enjoyed as an occasional treat — but eating fast food regularly means consuming a lot of hidden salt, saturated fat and added sugar. The key is balance! Aim to enjoy home-cooked or freshly prepared meals most of the time. 🏠

Sources: NHS UK Eat Well Guide · WHO Nutrition Guidelines · Hong Kong Department of Health

Sleep: Your Secret Superpower!

While you sleep, your brain is incredibly busy — sorting memories, processing emotions and helping your body grow! Sleep is not wasted time; it is absolutely essential. 🌙

👇 Tap any card to reveal an amazing fact!

📵
Screen time and sleep in Hong Kong ⚠️
Research shows that many children in Hong Kong spend significant time on screens before bed — and this is one of the biggest reasons for poor sleep quality! Screens emit blue light, which tells your brain it is still daytime and prevents you from feeling sleepy. The NHS recommends switching off all screens at least 1 hour before bedtime. Try reading a book, drawing or calm breathing instead. Your sleep will thank you! 📚

Sources: NHS UK · WHO · Healthier Together NHS · Hong Kong Department of Health

Why It All Connects!

Nutrition, exercise and sleep do not work alone — they are a powerful team! And they all connect to your mental wellbeing and happiness too. 🌈

🥦 Nutrition and Your Mood

Did you know that certain foods can actually affect how you feel? The NHS confirms that a balanced diet rich in vegetables, whole grains and protein supports healthy brain function and stable moods. Eating too much sugar causes energy spikes followed by crashes — leaving you feeling irritable, anxious or unfocused. 🍭

🏃 Exercise and Your Mind

Exercise is one of the most powerful natural mood boosters there is! When you are physically active, your brain releases chemicals called endorphins, which reduce stress and create feelings of happiness. Research from the WHO shows that regular physical activity is linked to lower anxiety and better emotional wellbeing in children. Even a short walk makes a big difference! 😊

😴 Sleep and Your Emotions

When you do not get enough sleep, it becomes much harder to manage your emotions and focus in class. The NHS highlights that poor sleep in children is linked to increased anxiety, low mood and difficulty with friendships and learning. A good night's sleep helps your brain process the events of the day — including any worries — so you wake up feeling balanced and refreshed. 🌙

⚖️ The ISF Value of Balance

At ISF, Balance is one of our most treasured values. True balance means caring for your whole self — your body, your mind and your emotions. When you eat well, move regularly and sleep enough, you are living in balance. This helps you bring your very best self to school, to your friendships and to everything you do. Balance is not about perfection — it is about small, positive choices every day. 🌿
